Common questions

Good to know before you book.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We come out, take a look, and give you a clear written quote with no obligation and no pushy follow-ups. You decide on your terms.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. BC Licensed (FM1112157) and fully insured with $3M liability, and every job is backed by a craftsmanship warranty of up to 9 years.

What does an interior repaint cost?

It depends on the number of rooms, the wall condition and how much prep is involved, which is why we quote in person instead of throwing out a number that creeps up later. We're not the cheapest, but the price we quote is the price you pay, and it's done once.

Do you move furniture and cover floors?

Yes. We shift and cover furniture, protect your floors, and mask everything that isn't getting painted. You come home to fresh walls, not a mess.

How long will my rooms be out of action?

It depends on the size of the job, but you'll know the full timeline before we start, no surprises. We work room by room where we can, so you can keep living in your home while we go.

How soon can you start?

Once you're happy with the quote we confirm a start date and tell you what to expect ahead of time, so nothing feels last-minute. We book up fast, so earlier is better.

Do you cover my town?

We paint across Kelowna, West Kelowna, Lake Country, Peachland, Vernon and nearby. Pick your town on the form and we'll confirm.
