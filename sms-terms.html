What the messages are for

Companion Painting sends text messages to people who have given us their mobile number and asked us to get in touch. Messages relate to your estimate, your booking, updates while the work is underway, and a follow up after the job is finished. We may also ask how the job went and invite you to leave a review.

How you opt in

You opt in by giving us your mobile number and submitting a form on this website, by texting us first, or by giving your number to us in person or over the phone and asking us to follow up. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on your enquiry and your job. Most customers receive a small number of messages relating to their own estimate or booking. We do not send recurring marketing blasts.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. Companion Painting does not charge you for these messages, but your mobile carrier may, depending on your plan.

How to stop

Reply STOP to any message to opt out. You will receive one confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, and we will not text you again unless you opt back in.

Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us directly on (778) 946-5773 or at taylor@companionpainting.ca.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery is not guaranteed and can be affected by your network or handset.

Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy for the full detail on how we handle your information.
